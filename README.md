# 🇮🇹 Italian Piazza – 3D CAD Design Project

### From Conceptual Modeling to Detailed Engineering Design

A 3D modeling project focused on designing an Italian piazza, combining architectural creativity, spatial planning, and computer-aided design (CAD). The project began with an initial conceptual model in **SketchUp** and was further developed using **Autodesk Inventor** to explore more detailed engineering and 3D modeling techniques.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project was to recreate an Italian piazza through 3D modeling, capturing the architectural elements, layout, and overall atmosphere of a traditional Italian public square.

Rather than stopping at an initial visual concept, I developed the design further in Autodesk Inventor, gaining experience working across different CAD platforms and translating a conceptual design into a more detailed 3D model.

## 🛠️ Software & Technologies

- **SketchUp** – Initial 3D modeling, architectural layout, and conceptual design.
- **Autodesk Inventor** – Further development, 3D modeling, and detailed design work.

## ⚙️ Design Process

1. **Concept Development:** Established the overall layout and visual direction of the Italian piazza.
2. **Initial Modeling:** Created the original architectural concept in SketchUp, developing the arrangement of structures and surrounding spaces.
3. **Design Development:** Transitioned the project into Autodesk Inventor to further develop the model.
4. **Refinement:** Worked on the geometry, proportions, and individual architectural elements to improve the overall design.

## 🎯 Skills Developed

- 3D Modeling and CAD
- Architectural Design
- Spatial Planning
- Engineering Visualization
- Cross-Platform Design Development
- Technical Problem-Solving

## 💡 Project Takeaway

This project strengthened my understanding of how digital modeling can be used to turn an initial concept into a developed design. It also gave me experience working with multiple CAD platforms and exploring the relationship between creativity, engineering, and technical design.
